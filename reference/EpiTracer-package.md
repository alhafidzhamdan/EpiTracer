# EpiTracer: Calling and Visualising Episomal Extrachromosomal DNA from WGS

Tools for detecting episomal extrachromosomal DNA (ecDNA) amplicons
(ecDNAs formed by simple DNA excision events, instead of complex dsDNA
breaks i.e. chromothripsis) from whole-genome sequencing data and for
visualising the structural rearrangements underlying focal
amplifications. The episomal ecDNA caller identifies duplication
breakpoints at amplicon boundaries, checks for excision scars, and
classifies amplicons as episomal in origin. Two complementary plotters
visualise the rearrangements: a linear allele-specific copy-number and
structural-variant viewer across one or more loci, and a reconstruction
plotter that stratifies junctions by read support (variant fraction) and
rebuilds a focal amplicon wave by wave. The linear viewer can optionally
add a stacked SNV panel. Copy-number and structural variant inputs are
expected from PURPLE (Hartwig Medical Foundation pipeline).
